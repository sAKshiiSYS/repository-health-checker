# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in Repository Health Checker a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of their background, experience level, or identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Welcoming beginners and helping them learn.
- Giving constructive feedback.
- Accepting feedback and suggestions gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or disrespectful comments.
- Trolling or deliberately disrupting discussions.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that makes participation hostile or unsafe.

## Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards fairly.

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to address unacceptable behavior.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainers through an appropriate private communication channel.

Please provide relevant context so the maintainers can understand and address the concern.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including GitHub Issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community spaces used for project collaboration.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reports fairly and take appropriate action based on the circumstances.

## Attribution

This is a project-specific Code of Conduct written for Repository Health Checker.
